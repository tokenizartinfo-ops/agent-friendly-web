**N05 / Important — The new timeout layer can leave its separately sessioned grandchild alive.** outer.mjs:57–61 starts Python in a detached group and kills only that group after 5 s / 120 s. boundary.py:58–60 and controls-boundary.py:58–60 start their own Node in a NEW session and start their inner clocks after spawn, for the same 5 s / 120 s.
