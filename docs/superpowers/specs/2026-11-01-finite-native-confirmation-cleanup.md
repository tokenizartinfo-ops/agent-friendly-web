## Independent exact QA finalizer
Proposed scripts/run-qa-finite-trial-finalizer.mjs runs in a separate GHA job/process with its own admitted original R1 and immutable exact trial pins, started and acknowledged ready BEFORE arm. It is not nominal finally and is not cancelled with nominal execution. Finalizer polls within R1 for canary closed, physical stop/no-admission, guard completion and exact nominal Workflow terminal state. It must not require its own GHA job terminal before its POST. It then restores QA, independently reads all closed profiles/namespaces/terminal relevant Workflows and reports exact evidence. R1 expiry gives admin-pending, never extension or blind restore. A source-bound manifest and durable effect claim survive process restart; restart cannot regain nominal authority.

Existing AFW_QA_CLOSED_TOKEN is QA-limited custody: qualify its actual metadata/expiry for this new fixed use, do not infer canary permission. Canary publisher is separately custodied and must be qualified for the exact allowlisted deployment; do not expose it to nominal code or GHA by convenience. Scoped close uses fixed DO binding, not a broad CF token. No new key/role is assumed.

Guard and executor share QA failure domain; guard is independent of canary and nominal runner, not of QA failure. External GHA finalizer has a separate failure domain but availability/scheduling is not guaranteed. Simultaneous host/finalizer failure or missing physical proof leaves uncertainty; no permanent guard or atomic remote-cancel claim.
